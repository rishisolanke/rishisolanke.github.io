Featured Project
Sati. Built from scratch.
A cross-platform Buddhist meditation app for iOS, Android, and Web. Zero prior mobile experience. Just kept building until it worked.

Sati is a meditation and Buddhist practice app designed to be a kalyana mitta (spiritual friend). It serves practitioners across five Buddhist traditions with authentic content, honest data, and technology that stays out of the way.

The app includes a meditation timer, AI practice companion, 24 guided sessions, a Pali chanting library with programmatic instrument rhythms via Web Audio API, full Dhammapada text (26 chapters), daily practice tracking, retreat mode, courses, and a live sangha (community) feature.

Solo-built end-to-end: product vision, UX design, frontend, backend, AI integration, audio engineering, content curation, native app packaging, and store compliance. 107 source files, 18,460 lines of JS, 449 audio files, 30 automated tests.
