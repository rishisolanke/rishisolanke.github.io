Featured Project
Sati. Built from scratch.
A cross-platform Buddhist meditation app for iOS, Android, and Web. No prior mobile experience. Just kept building until it worked.

Sati is a meditation and Buddhist practice app designed to be a kalyana mitta (spiritual friend). It serves practitioners across five Buddhist traditions with real content, honest data, and technology that stays out of the way.

Includes a meditation timer, AI practice companion powered by Claude API, 24 guided sessions, a Pali chanting library with instrument rhythms built using Web Audio API, the full Dhammapada (26 chapters, 423 verses), daily practice tracking, retreat mode, courses, and a live sangha feature.

Solo-built end-to-end: product vision, UX design, frontend, backend, AI integration, audio engineering, content curation, native packaging, and store compliance. 107 source files, 18,460 lines of JS, 449 audio files, 30 automated tests. No team, no designer, just me.
