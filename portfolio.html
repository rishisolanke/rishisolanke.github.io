Questions

A few things people usually ask.

I already have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

Social is great, but you don't own it, and 84% of people trust an actual website more than a social page alone. A site is the one spot online that's fully yours, shows up on Google, and keeps working even when the algorithm changes.

Do I even need a website?

If people can look you up, yes. 62% will skip a business they can't find online, and there are over a billion "near me" searches a month. A simple site is often the difference between getting the call and losing it to the shop that showed up.

Why not just build it myself on Wix?

You can, and for some people that's the right call. But it eats your time, and the free templates tend to look like free templates. I handle the whole thing so you can run your business, and it comes out looking like yours, not a stock theme.

What if I don't like it? Do I really not pay upfront?

Right, you don't pay until it's live and you're happy. If the first design misses, I redo it at no cost. The flat price we agree on is the only number you'll see.

Do I own my website?

Yes, completely. The code and domain are yours. No lock-in, no monthly fee required to keep it running.

Who pays for hosting?

Hosting itself is usually free. The only real cost is a domain name, typically $12 to $15 a year, paid directly to the registrar, not to me.

How fast can we start?

Usually within a day or two of our first call. Builds take days, not weeks, once we agree on scope.

Why so cheap? What's the catch?

No catch. I'm just starting out taking on local businesses, so my prices reflect that while I build a track record. You get the same care either way.
