Our Beans

We roast small, and we roast often.

Every bag is roasted a few blocks from where you're standing, in batches small enough to taste as they go. We buy seasonally, pay fairly, and keep the roast light enough to let the origin do the talking.

Beans go on the shelf the same week they're roasted. If it's older than that, it's not for sale. It's for us.
